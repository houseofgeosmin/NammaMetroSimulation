Rider reports: real arrival times

Standing on a platform? Pick your station and the train direction, then tap the button the moment a train arrives. Your report is compared with the timetable and saved for everyone, so the simulator can learn how late or early trains really run.

At-

Every train runs on the BMRCL timetable as transcribed in the unofficial community GTFS feed (valid 17 Aug 2026 to 17 Aug 2027). BMRCL publishes terminal times only, so times at stations between the terminals are modelled and can differ from the platform display by a minute or two. Trains are drawn as six-car sets like the real ones, but longer than true scale so they stay visible. Lines and stations are drawn at their real coordinates, without street tiles, and the 3D tilt is only a viewing angle. Pink Line trains are not in this feed yet. Planned lines (Pink, Blue, Orange, Grey and Sarjapur to Hebbal) can be switched on from the last card. They show only terminals and junctions, in dashed lines, with underground parts dotted. Positions of stations that do not exist yet are approximate and the straight links between them are schematic.
